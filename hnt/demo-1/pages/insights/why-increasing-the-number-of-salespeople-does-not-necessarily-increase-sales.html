Why Increasing the Number of Salespeople Does Not Necessarily Increase Sales

By Ajit Jain · 2 min read

When a business wants to grow, adding salespeople is an obvious option. But if territory potential, product-market fit or sales management is weak, new hires may simply increase cost and complexity.

Before recruiting, examine the current sales system. Are territories sized appropriately? Are salespeople spending time with the right customers? Is there enough demand in the market? Are quotations converting? Do technical support, delivery and service teams have capacity to handle additional business?

Look at productivity by salesperson and territory, not only total revenue. Identify whether the constraint is coverage, capability, customer access or internal execution. Sometimes a new person is needed. In other cases, better account planning, application selling or distributor development may produce more growth at lower cost.

If recruitment is the answer, define the role and territory clearly. Give new employees realistic ramp-up expectations and practical support.

The takeawayMore people create more capacity only when the business has a clear plan for using that capacity. Growth comes from a productive sales system, not headcount alone.

— Ajit
