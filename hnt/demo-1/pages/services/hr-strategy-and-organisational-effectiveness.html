HR Strategy & Organisational Effectiveness

Make people practices work harder for the business.

HR creates greater value when its priorities are connected to the business agenda. Recruitment, performance management, rewards, engagement, leadership development and succession should help the organisation execute its strategy—not operate as disconnected processes.

Head & Tail Asia works with business and HR leaders to diagnose people and organisation challenges, identify practical improvements and support implementation.

Areas of intervention

  • Align HR priorities and people plans with business strategy.
  • Review organisation structure, role clarity, decision rights and interfaces.
  • Strengthen performance management, goal setting and review processes.
  • Review recognition, rewards and incentive approaches in line with business priorities.
  • Diagnose employee engagement and attrition concerns and identify actionable drivers.
  • Build leadership competency frameworks and development plans.
  • Strengthen talent reviews, high-potential development and succession planning.
  • Improve recruitment processes and role-specific assessment approaches.
  • Support HR process design, HRIS adoption and practical people analytics where appropriate.
  • Build stronger working relationships between HR, sales, operations and other business functions.

What progress can look like

Clearer accountability, more useful people processes, better alignment between HR and business leaders, and stronger visibility of the people issues that affect execution.
