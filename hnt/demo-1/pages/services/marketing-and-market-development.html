Marketing & Market Development

Connect your market story to customer needs and sales action.

Marketing is most valuable when it helps the business reach the right customers, communicate a relevant value proposition and create opportunities that sales teams can convert. In technical and industrial markets, this requires close coordination between product knowledge, customer applications and commercial priorities.

Areas of intervention

  • Clarify market positioning, product messaging and differentiation.
  • Identify priority sectors, customer groups and applications.
  • Align marketing plans and campaigns with sales priorities.
  • Design customer engagement initiatives such as technical sessions, events, webinars and account reviews.
  • Improve coordination between sales, marketing, product and technical teams.
  • Support brand and market development plans linked to business objectives.
  • Identify opportunities to develop new products, markets and geographies in collaboration with the client team.

What progress can look like

A clearer market proposition, more focused marketing effort, stronger sales-marketing coordination and customer engagement that supports commercial priorities.
