Sales Effectiveness & Growth Acceleration

Build a sales process that is disciplined, customer-focused and repeatable.

In industrial B2B markets, strong products and technical expertise do not automatically translate into orders. Sales performance depends on customer understanding, opportunity quality, disciplined follow-through, effective negotiation, sound forecasting and capable sales leadership.

We help businesses identify where sales effectiveness is breaking down and strengthen the practices that improve execution.

Areas of intervention

  • Review the sales process from lead generation to order closure.
  • Improve pipeline creation, qualification, opportunity management and conversion.
  • Strengthen sales forecasting, review meetings, KPIs and dashboards.
  • Build capability in consultative selling, value-added selling, negotiation and customer relationship management.
  • Develop key account management, customer profiling and account-growth plans.
  • Coach sales managers in field coaching, feedback, goal setting and performance conversations.
  • Align sales goals, incentives, recognition and performance management with business priorities.
  • Build a common sales philosophy and consistent ways of working across the team.
  • Explore practical applications of digital tools and AI in sales where they fit the business need.

How we work

Depending on the requirement, the intervention may combine competency assessment, gap analysis, workshops, field visits, individual coaching, sales playbooks and regular performance reviews.

What progress can look like

A healthier pipeline, better opportunity conversion, more reliable forecasts, stronger account development and improved alignment between sales activity and business targets. The measures are agreed with the client at the outset.
