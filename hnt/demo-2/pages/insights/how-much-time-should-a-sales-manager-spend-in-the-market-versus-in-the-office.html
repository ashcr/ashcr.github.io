How Much Time Should a Sales Manager Spend in the Market Versus in the Office?

Ajit Jain · 2 min read

Sales managers often struggle to balance customer visits with reviews, reports, approvals and internal meetings. Spend too much time in the office and they lose touch with the market. Spend every day travelling and important planning and coaching work gets postponed.

There is no universal percentage that suits every business. The right balance depends on the maturity of the team, market conditions, geography and the manager's responsibilities. What matters is that time in the market has a purpose.

Plan visits around specific objectives: understand a customer problem, coach a salesperson, support a strategic opportunity or investigate a competitor. Avoid joining visits simply to show presence. Equally, protect time for pipeline reviews, territory planning and developing people.

Review the calendar regularly. If administrative work is consuming most of the week, simplify reporting and delegate where possible. If customer visits are not producing insight or progress, change how they are planned.

The takeaway

A sales manager should be visible in the market and effective inside the organisation. Both responsibilities matter.
