Leadership Coaching & Development

Develop leaders who think clearly, build capability and deliver.

Leadership development is most effective when it is connected to the real challenges leaders face. A newly promoted manager, an experienced leader facing performance issues or a high-potential employee preparing for a larger role may each need a different form of support.

At Head & Tail Asia, coaching is integrated with business and organisational interventions. It helps leaders turn insight into action while strengthening ownership, judgement, adaptability and the ability to develop others.

Situations where coaching can help

  • A capable manager is not translating potential into consistent performance.
  • An individual contributor has moved into a people-leadership role and needs support with the transition.
  • A sales leader needs to strengthen team performance, forecasting discipline or talent retention.
  • A leader needs to navigate change, cross-functional tension or greater business complexity.
  • A high-potential employee is preparing for a critical role or expanded responsibility.
  • HR and business leaders need to work together more effectively on a key organisational priority.

Areas of focus

  • Leadership effectiveness, self-awareness and communication.
  • Commercial thinking, decision-making and accountability.
  • Manager-as-coach capability and team development.
  • Goal setting, performance conversations and feedback.
  • Transition into leadership and readiness for broader responsibilities.
  • Coaching culture and continuous improvement in manufacturing environments.

A practical, context-led approach

The coaching approach is agreed around the coachee's role, business context and development goals. Where appropriate, it can be supported by stakeholder input, competency assessment, 360-degree feedback, goal reviews and discussions with the organisation's sponsor, with confidentiality and boundaries agreed in advance.

Our aim is not to make coaching a conversation separate from work. It is to help leaders think and act more effectively in the work that matters.
