FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What does Head & Tail Asia do?

We are a boutique, business-led management intervention firm helping industrial B2B manufacturers and engineering businesses connect business strategy, sales effectiveness, leadership and people practices to improve execution.

How are you different from a traditional consulting firm?

We focus on practical intervention and implementation, not advice alone. We work with leaders and teams to diagnose the issue, co-design a solution, support action and review progress against agreed outcomes.

Do you work only on Sales or HR?

No. Our differentiator is the connection between business growth, sales execution and people strategy. We can work on a focused issue or an integrated challenge that crosses these areas.

Which organisations do you work with?

Our primary focus is industrial B2B manufacturers and engineering businesses. The scope and approach are tailored to each organisation's context, priorities and readiness.

Can you help with a specific issue rather than a large transformation programme?

Yes. An engagement can be focused on a clearly defined challenge, such as pipeline effectiveness, distributor performance, leadership transition or a performance-management process. We first agree the issue and desired outcome.

Do you provide leadership coaching?

Yes. Coaching is integrated with our broader interventions and can support leaders dealing with performance, role transitions, team development, change or business challenges. The scope and confidentiality arrangements are agreed upfront.

Can you work with our existing HR and sales teams?

Yes. Our approach is collaborative. We aim to build internal capability and ownership, working alongside existing teams rather than replacing their responsibility.

How do you measure success?

Measures depend on the challenge. They may include agreed sales-process indicators, pipeline quality, conversion, forecast accuracy, role clarity, completion of development actions or progress on implementation milestones. Measures are agreed with the client at the beginning.

How long does an intervention take?

There is no single standard duration. It depends on the problem, scope, stakeholders and level of implementation support required. We propose a practical scope after an initial discussion.

How do we get started?

Email ajit.jain.gsti@gmail.com with a brief description of your business challenge. We can then arrange an initial conversation to understand the context and consider whether an intervention would be useful.

Have a business challenge that needs more than a recommendation?

Let's understand what is holding performance back and identify the intervention that can move it forward.
