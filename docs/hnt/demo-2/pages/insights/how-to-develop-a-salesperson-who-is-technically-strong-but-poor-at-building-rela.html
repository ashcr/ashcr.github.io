Managing Sales Teams

How to Develop a Salesperson Who Is Technically Strong but Poor at Building Relationships

Ajit Jain · 2 min read

In industrial markets, technical knowledge is valuable. A salesperson who understands applications, specifications and production processes can earn credibility quickly. But technical expertise alone may not uncover customer priorities or build the relationships needed to win business.

Start by identifying the specific gap. Does the salesperson talk too much about product features? Do they struggle to ask open questions, listen patiently or connect with non-technical stakeholders? Do they avoid informal conversations or fail to follow up?

Development should happen in real customer situations. Before a visit, practise a few questions about the customer's operations, costs and challenges. During the visit, encourage the salesperson to listen and explore before presenting a solution. Afterwards, discuss what they learned about the people and the business.

Pair them with a colleague who is strong in relationship-building, and provide feedback on observable behaviour.

The takeaway

The goal is not to turn a technical expert into a smooth talker. It is to help them use technical knowledge in a way that customers understand, trust and value.
