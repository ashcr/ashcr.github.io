When a Customer Keeps Asking for Quotations but Never Places an Order

Ajit Jain · 2 min read

Some customers request quotation after quotation but never move forward. They may be comparing suppliers, checking market prices or using your offer to negotiate with an existing vendor. Salespeople often keep responding because they hope the next enquiry will become a real order.

Before preparing another detailed proposal, qualify the opportunity. Ask what application the quotation is for, what quantity is expected, when the decision will be made and who will evaluate the offer. Find out whether there is an approved budget or a genuine project. These are reasonable business questions, not signs of poor service.

If the customer is only benchmarking, decide how much effort is appropriate. You can provide indicative pricing where suitable, while reserving detailed engineering, trials and management time for qualified opportunities.

Track repeated quotation requests and conversion rates. A high volume of quotations may look like activity but can hide wasted effort.

The takeaway

A good sales team responds promptly, but it also protects its time for opportunities where it can create real value.
