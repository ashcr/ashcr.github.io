Channel & Distributors

Should You Appoint More Distributors or Develop Your Existing Ones?

Ajit Jain · 2 min read

When sales growth slows, appointing more distributors can seem like the quickest solution. More names on the network may create the impression of wider coverage. But additional partners do not automatically mean more customers, better service or higher sales.

Before expanding, examine the performance of the current network. Are distributors covering the agreed territory? Do they have the right customer relationships, stock and technical capability? Are there clear gaps in geography or applications? Is poor performance caused by limited market potential or by insufficient support?

Sometimes existing distributors can grow significantly through better planning, training, joint customer visits and clearer targets. In other cases, the market is underserved and a new partner is necessary. Avoid creating overlap that leads to price competition and channel conflict.

Make the decision based on territory potential, customer access and the cost of serving the market. Define the role of each distributor and review performance regularly.

The takeaway

The objective is not to have the largest distribution network. It is to have the right network, with partners who can develop profitable and sustainable business.
